The Controller may process certain personal data to comply with applicable legal obligations, such as:

In cases where processing is not necessary for contract performance or legal obligations, the user’s consent will be requested, for example:

  • Creating individualised profiles based on physical indicators.
  • Generating personalised nutritional recommendations.
  • Analysing the user’s physical condition according to their goals.

Processing may be based on the Controller’s legitimate interest in the following cases, provided it does not override the user’s interests or rights:

  • Technical security and detection of unauthorised access.

A balancing test has been carried out demonstrating the prevalence of the Controller’s legitimate interest.
